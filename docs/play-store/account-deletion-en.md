# Delete your Timing account

To request deletion of your Timing account and associated personal data, email joaovictorjorgealmeidabenevide@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Timing account with the subject **Timing account deletion request**.

Include your Timing display name and friend code, if available. Do not include your password or authentication codes.

After verifying that the request belongs to the account holder, we will delete the account and personal data associated with it, including profile information, private plans, tasks, schedules, notes, and focus-history records. Group or friend records may be anonymized or removed as needed to preserve the integrity of other users' records. Limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or encrypted backup rotation.

We will acknowledge the request and provide an expected completion date by email.
