## Full description

Your time. Your progress.

Timing brings focus, routines, and personal growth together in one place. Plan what matters, track your activities, and turn goals into consistent habits.

With Timing, you can:

• Run focused sessions with breaks and reminders.
• Organize daily goals and track your streaks.
• Plan schedules and recurring commitments.
• Track studying, reading, exercise, and hobbies.
• Review your progress by day, week, or month.
• Create groups and compare progress with friends.
• Take notes during focus sessions.
• Customize notifications, language, and focus mode.

Use Timing to study, read, train, or improve any activity that depends on consistency. You choose the goal; the app helps you see every step forward.

Start at your own pace and keep track of everything you achieve.
