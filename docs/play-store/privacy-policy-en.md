# Timing Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Timing is a focus, planning, goal-tracking, and shared-progress application. This Privacy Policy explains what information Timing processes, why it is processed, and the choices available to users.

## Information we process

Depending on the features you use, Timing may process:

- Account information provided through Google sign-in, including your name, email address, Google account identifier, and profile image.
- Profile information you choose to add, such as a nickname, phone number, date of birth, profile photo, avatar, language, and appearance preferences.
- Content you create in the app, including subjects, goals, tasks, schedules, notes, focus-session records, reading progress, and achievement progress.
- Social and group information, including friend relationships, invitations, group memberships, group progress, and images you intentionally share in a group.
- Notification and focus-lock preferences stored for app functionality.
- Technical information required to authenticate your account, synchronize data, and keep the service secure.

Timing does not sell personal information and does not display third-party advertising.

## How information is used

We use information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Synchronize your profile, plans, goals, progress, and social features across sessions.
- Provide focus timers, reminders, schedules, progress reports, achievements, groups, and friend features.
- Display information you choose to share with friends or group members.
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and support users.

## Service providers

Timing uses Google for account authentication and Supabase for authentication, database storage, and synchronization. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services and are governed by their own privacy terms.

## Photos and camera access

Camera or photo-library access is requested only when you choose to add a profile photo or share an image in a group. Timing does not access photos in the background. Images you choose may be stored with your account or group content so the selected feature can work.

## Notifications

Timing may request notification permission to show focus-timer and reminder notifications. Notification preferences can be changed in the app or in Android settings.

## Sharing with other users

When you use friends or groups, other participants may see information needed for those features, such as your display name, avatar or profile photo, relevant progress, and content you intentionally share. Do not share information that you do not want other participants to see.

## Data retention and deletion

Information is retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. You may request deletion of your Timing account and associated personal data by following the account-deletion instructions published for the app or by contacting joaovictorjorgealmeidabenevide@gmail.com.

Some information may remain for a limited period in encrypted backups or where retention is required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Content shared with other users may remain visible where removal would affect their records or where it has been independently saved by them.

## Security

Timing uses encrypted network connections and access controls intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Children

Timing is not directed to children under 13. If we learn that personal information from a child under 13 was collected without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

## International processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Timing changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
